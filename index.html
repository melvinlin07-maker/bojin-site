What the method is

What is facial bojin?

Facial bojin (撥筋) is a hands-on self-care method Yu-Ting teaches from Taiwan. You glide a rounded stainless steel bojin stick, or your fingertips, slowly along one line of the face or neck while your other hand steadies the skin. Each area takes about a minute per side.

Yu-Ting teaches three details: where you start, which way you move, and how much pressure you use. You want enough to reach the tension and never enough to hurt. It won't change your bone structure or erase a line, and it is educational self-care, not medical care.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

  • What is facial bojin (撥筋)?

    Facial bojin is a hands-on face and neck self-care method taught by Taiwan-based instructor Yu-Ting Lan. You glide a rounded stainless steel bojin stick, or your fingertips, slowly along one line of the face or neck while your other hand steadies the skin. It is educational self-care, not medical care.

  • How long does it take?

    About a minute per side for one area. That is the real practice time Yu-Ting teaches, not a promise of how fast you will see a change. Any change is gentle and temporary, and it varies from person to person.

  • Do I need to buy a bojin stick first?

    No. You can start with your fingertips, or with the gua sha stone you already own. The free demo, the quiz and the area guides cost nothing.

  • How is facial bojin different from gua sha?

    Gua sha usually sweeps a flat stone across a wider area. Bojin glides a rounded tip along one short line, in a set direction, while your other hand steadies the skin. They do different jobs, and you can use both.

  • Where should I start?

    Pick the area that bothers you most from the list at the top of this page. If you are not sure, the 1-minute Face Tension Profile checks five areas of your face and neck and shows your result on screen, with no email needed.
