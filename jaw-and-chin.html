What your jaw and chin are holding

Tight by evening. A chin that dimples when you aren’t even frowning. One side a little fuller than the other. Here’s what’s usually behind it, a 10-second check, and one thing to try tonight.

The short answer

Most of it comes from two small muscles. The chewing muscle at the corner of your jaw (the masseter) tightens when you clench, talk for hours, or chew mostly on one side. A small muscle at the point of the chin can stay tense even at rest, giving the skin a dimpled, pebbly look that many people call pebble chin. Gentle self-care can help both feel less held. Jaw pain, clicking, recent dental work, or a sudden change on one side of the face are for a dentist or doctor. Jaw joint disorders (TMD) affect about 5% of U.S. adults, twice as often women, according to the NIH.

Try it tonight

One gentle line along the jaw

Trace the lower edge of your jawbone with a fingertip: the line of bone that runs from your chin back toward your ear. Press just enough to feel the bone under your fingertip; it should never hurt. With a little cream, glide lightly along that edge, from beside the chin to the soft hollow just behind your earlobe. Keep it slow and comfortable, then look at both sides in the mirror.

“At first, almost no one can find the bone edge. They work in the wrong spot, because they’re afraid to press.”Yu-Ting Lan, on new students

Stop if anything aches or clicks. This is one line of a longer routine; the full method works the face in a set order.

Common questions

Jaw and chin questions

Can bojin help TMJ or jaw pain?

No. It isn’t a treatment for TMJ disorders or pain. Clicking, locking, or pain when you chew should be checked by a dentist. If your jaw simply feels tight, gentle work at comfortable pressure is a way to let it rest.

Can it get rid of jowls or a double chin?

No. It can’t change bone, fat, or support that has loosened over time. Some people find the jawline looks a little cleaner when tension and evening puffiness ease. That doesn’t last and varies from person to person.
