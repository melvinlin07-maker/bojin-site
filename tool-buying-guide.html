Three checks when it arrives

  1. Smooth edges: inspect all tips and edges for anything sharp or rough.
  2. No damage: check under light for nicks, burrs and seams. Set aside a damaged tool.
  3. Comfortable grip: check the hold and contact tip with M2. Do not force an awkward fit.
