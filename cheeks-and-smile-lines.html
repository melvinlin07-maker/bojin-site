Your concern · Cheeks & Smile Lines

Smile lines, softer cheeks, and what you can reach

The fold from your nose to your mouth looks deeper. Your cheeks seem to sit lower by evening or after a flight. Some of this no home method changes. Some of it you can reach.

Home›Your concern›Cheeks & Smile Lines

By Yu-Ting Lan · Taiwan-based bojin instructor · Updated November 23, 2026

The short answer

The smile line forms where the cheek meets the upper lip, and it deepens as cheek tissue settles with age. Nothing at home removes it. What gentle work can reach is the layer on top: tension from the smile muscles and the chewing muscle behind them, and the puffiness that gathers by evening or after travel. Easing those can make the fold look a little softer for a while. Swelling with pain or fever, or numbness, belongs with a doctor.

Try it tonight

One gentle line under the cheekbone

Find the lower edge of your cheekbone with a fingertip, starting beside your nose. With a little cream, glide lightly along that edge, from beside the nose out toward your ear. Keep it slow and comfortable, then compare both sides in the mirror.

“Most people can’t find the cheekbone line at first. They end up a little too low or too high, and if you miss that line, you won’t feel much from it.”Yu-Ting Lan, on new students

Stop if anything aches. Skip it if your sinuses are sore or you’ve had filler in the area recently. This is one line of a longer routine.

Common questions

Cheek and smile line questions

Are smile lines permanent?

The fold is part of every face and deepens with age; no home method removes it. What can change is how sharp it looks when the cheeks and jaw are tense or puffy. Any softening doesn’t last and varies from person to person.

Can bojin replace filler?

No. Filler adds volume; gentle work doesn’t. If you have filler, wait as long as your provider advises, and keep the pressure light afterward.
