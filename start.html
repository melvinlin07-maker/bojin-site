New to bojin? Here’s where to start.

Nothing to buy. Three steps, in order: find your area, learn how the method works, then practice safely. A first session takes about a minute per side, and about twice a week is enough.

Your first week

What your first week looks like

How often
About twice a week, with rest days between.
How long
About a minute per area, per side. Three slow passes.
How many areas
Start with one.
What to use
Your fingertips and a little oil or cream. A tool can come later.
When to stop
If it hurts, stings or leaves a mark.

These are the numbers Yu-Ting teaches in her classes. This is self-care, not medical care; if you have a skin or health condition, check with your doctor first.
