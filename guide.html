Anatomical Roadmap

The 5 tension zones mapped in your guide

Rather than rubbing skin blindly, the Bojin Method traces the deeper fascial pathways where daily posture, screens, and clenching quietly settle.

Zone 01

Brow & Forehead

Screen concentration, squinting, and furrowing glue the frontalis muscle to the cranial fascia, creating a sensation of heavy, tired brows.

Zone 02

Eye Contour & Temples

Orbicularis oculi tightness restricts local fluid movement, leading to morning puffiness, hollow fatigue, and temporal tightness by 4 PM.

Zone 03

Mid-Face & Cheek

Zygomatic muscles hold habitual expressions. When fascia stiffens here, cheek tissue pulls inward, deepening the nasolabial fold.

Zone 04

Jaw & Masseter

Nighttime clenching and daytime stress turn the masseter into a rock-hard knot, squaring the lower face and dragging the jawline downward.

Zone 05

Submandibular & Neck

Platysma tension and forward-head posture trap lymphatic fluid under the chin, muting the clean transition between jaw and neck.

The Golden Rule: The 60-Second One-Side Test

Never work both sides simultaneously on your first try. When you release only the right side for 60 to 90 seconds, you create an unmistakable baseline. Look in the mirror: compare eye aperture, brow height, and jaw softness against your untouched left side. Your own face is your best teacher.
