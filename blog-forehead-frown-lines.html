Frown lines and a tense brow: what to notice before trying Bojin

By Yu-Ting Lan, from Taiwan · 5 min read · Updated October 23, 2026

The line between your brows catches your eye. The held feeling above them is harder to describe. When both show up together, it is tempting to treat them as one problem. You can notice each without having to fix your face tonight.

A line and a feeling are different observations

A visible crease does not tell you which tissue needs work, how deeply a tool should go, or whether a technique is suitable for you. It is a place to begin a question, not a map for pressing.

Skin aging and repeated facial expressions can contribute to visible lines, as the American Academy of Dermatology explains. That general information does not establish that Bojin removes them. The feeling you describe as tightness deserves its own attention, without a promise that the line will change.

What you can actually see in the brow demonstration

In the original lesson, the teacher completes a small circle above the brow before moving outward. The comparison clip makes that different from a side-to-side sway. This is a visible movement distinction, rather than proof that a particular deep muscle has released.

The supporting hand is another part of the lesson. Watch how the two hands work together instead of copying the tool hand alone. The article is a way into that detail, not a replacement for the preparation, suitability information, and full guided lesson.

Watch one detail before trying to copy the whole movement

Start by watching the comparison without a tool in your hand. Can you see where one circle finishes and the outward move begins? Then look at what the other hand is doing. You do not need to decide on a routine to notice the difference.

The brow guide has a location illustration, teacher excerpts, a movement comparison, and preparation reminders. Its sections help you explore a detail; they are not six new treatment steps or a neck-drainage routine.

What this article can help you decide

You can decide whether you want a closer look at the method and whether the teaching is clear enough to follow. A subjective sense of comfort, if you experience it, is not a measurement of tissue depth, lasting lifting, or increased skincare absorption.

There is no wrinkle-removal promise or fixed daily schedule here. If your main goal is changing persistent lines, a qualified skin professional can discuss that concern separately from learning facial self-care.

Before considering practice

The course’s opening lesson excludes people with glaucoma, retinal problems, high myopia, or recent eye surgery. For recent eye-area injections or inflammation, follow the course’s advice to wait and consult your doctor first. The course also says to stop for sharp pain or visual changes.

Read the preparation and suitability reminders and the full course safety information before practising. These notes are not a complete medical screening.
