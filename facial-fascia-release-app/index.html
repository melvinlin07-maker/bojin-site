Before you begin

This is a cosmetic self-care demo, not treatment for swelling or a medical condition. Skip irritated or broken skin and unexplained, painful, or sudden swelling. If you have had a recent procedure or have a condition affecting safe massage, ask your clinician first.

Use very light, comfortable contact. Never press on the eyes or the front of the neck. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness, or irritation.
