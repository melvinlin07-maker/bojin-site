A few quick questions

Do I need any special tools?

No tool is needed to watch. The 1:18 demonstration uses a rounded stainless steel stick, but you can gently practice with your fingertips and supporting hand tonight while waiting for your tool. Stay above the brow bone, avoid the eyelid and eyeball, and stop if uncomfortable.

How is Bojin different from Gua Sha?

Bojin and Gua Sha are different practices. This demonstration focuses on small, slow movements, tool handling, and a supporting hand. Watch the technique before deciding whether to try it.

Is this suitable if I’ve never done anything like this?

Yes, it’s designed for complete beginners. Everything is gentle and slow, with no rubbing and no skin pulling.

How long until I see a difference?

There is no promised timeline or visible result. Use the video to learn the movement and notice your experience, without trying to force a change.

Is it safe?

It’s a gentle, low-pressure routine. It isn’t medical treatment. If you have a skin or health condition, check with your doctor first.
