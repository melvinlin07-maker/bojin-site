A few quick questions

Do I need any special tools?

No tool is needed to watch. The demo uses a rounded stainless steel stick, and you can try the same step tonight with your fingertips. Stay above the brow bone, avoid the eyelid and eyeball, and stop if it’s uncomfortable.

How long does it take?

The demo runs 1 minute 18 seconds. When you try the step yourself, it takes about a minute per side.

How is bojin different from gua sha?

Gua sha usually sweeps a flat stone across a wide area. Bojin works one small spot at a time, here small circles just above the brow, while your other hand steadies the skin. They do different jobs.

Is this suitable if I’ve never done anything like this?

Yes. The demo is made for complete beginners: one step, slow and gentle, with no rubbing or pulling.

How long until I see a difference?

There’s no promised timeline or visible result. Use the video to learn the movement and notice how your face feels, without trying to force a change.

Is it safe?

It’s a gentle, low-pressure step. It isn’t medical care. If you have a skin or health condition, check with your doctor first.
